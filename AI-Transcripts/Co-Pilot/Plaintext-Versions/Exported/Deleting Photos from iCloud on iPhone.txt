How can I delete photos stored in iCloud on my iPhone?
## Copilot
[1. support.apple.com](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104967?utm_source=copilot.com)  
[2. discussions.apple.com](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/254971149?utm_source=copilot.com)  

To delete photos stored in iCloud on your iPhone, follow these steps:

Open the Photos app on your iPhone.[1]
Select the photo(s) you want to delete.[1]
Tap the Trash button (it looks like a trash can) and then tap Delete Photo.[1]
Go to the Recently Deleted album (you can find it under Utilities when you swipe up in the Photos app).[1]
Select the photo(s) you want to permanently delete and tap Delete.[1]
If you use iCloud Photos, deleting photos from your iPhone will also remove them from iCloud and any other devices connected to the same Apple account.[2]

Got it! To delete photos from iCloud to free up storage without affecting your iPhone, follow these steps:

Open Settings on your iPhone.[1]
Tap your Apple ID at the top, then tap iCloud.[1]
Tap Manage Storage.[1]
Under Photos, tap Edit.[1]
Select the photos you want to delete and tap Delete.[1]
This will remove the selected photos from iCloud, freeing up storage space.[1]
